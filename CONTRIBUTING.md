# Contributing

Contributions are welcome to either template (law firm or SMB), particularly:

- **Corrections to Appendix B** — updated or newly issued ethics opinions, court rules, and standing orders (law firm template), or updated statutes, regulations, and enforcement developments (SMB template).
- **New jurisdictions and industries** — Appendix B entries for states, countries, or regulated sectors not yet covered, following the existing format.
- **Clarity improvements** — fixes to typos, formatting, or ambiguous wording that do not change a policy's substance.
- **Governance resources** — corrections or additions to the framework guidance (NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001) in the README and Customization Guide.
- **Translations and adaptations** — versions for other countries' legal regimes (open an issue first to discuss structure).

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue describing the change and citing the relevant authority (for Appendix B changes, link to the opinion or rule).
2. For text changes, submit a pull request against the Markdown files. Maintainers will regenerate the .docx and .pdf versions; please do not submit changes to the binary files.
3. Substantive policy changes (changing what the policy requires, rather than correcting or clarifying it) are at the maintainers' discretion and may be declined to keep the template stable.

## Ground rules

- By contributing, you agree your contribution is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](LICENSE), the same terms as the rest of the repository.
- Contributions must not include confidential or client-identifying information.
- Nothing submitted to or accepted into this repository is legal advice, and contributing does not create an attorney-client relationship with Falcon Rappaport & Berkman LLP or any contributor.
